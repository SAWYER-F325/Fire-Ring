**改进**
- 快捷键更简单：只保留「打开设置」一个全局快捷键，旁边加了开关。关掉后快捷键暂时不起作用，录好的组合键会保留，再打开就能用。位置在「设置 → 通用 → 快捷键」。
- 「打开菜单栏面板」的快捷键去掉了。刘海展开后顶部的「面板」「设置」按钮照常可用。

已安装旧版本的用户：打开菜单栏面板，底部会出现「有新版本」，点进去下载并安装即可；也可以直接下载下面的 dmg 重新安装。

**Improvements**
- Simpler shortcut: there is now one global shortcut, to open Settings, with a switch beside it. Turning it off pauses the shortcut but keeps the keys you recorded. Find it under Settings → General → Shortcuts.
- The shortcut for the menu-bar panel is gone. The “Panel” and “Settings” buttons in the expanded notch still work as before.

On an older version: open the menu-bar panel and click “New version” at the bottom to update, or download the dmg below.
