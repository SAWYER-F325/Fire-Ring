**新功能**
- 新增三个工具：Antigravity、Cursor、Grok（命令行和桌面版）。在设置的「连接」里连上即可，刘海和菜单栏面板会显示它们的任务和本机用量。感谢 Shy-Plus 贡献。

**改进**
- 提醒更可靠：声音和系统通知改由 App 直接发出，刘海收起或被全屏 App 盖住时也能按时提醒。运行日志会记下每一次提醒。
- 刘海和菜单栏面板只占看得见的区域，不再有一块透明区域挡在别的软件上面。
- 动画更顺：刘海展开时不再闪一下，「全部清除」时滑动别的页面不再卡顿。
- 去掉了首次打开的欢迎动画和引导，第一次打开直接来到设置的「连接」。

**New**
- Three more tools: Antigravity, Cursor and Grok (CLI and desktop). Connect them under Settings → Connections, and their tasks and local usage show up in the notch and the menu-bar panel. Thanks to Shy-Plus for the contribution.
- Choose which usage limits the menu-bar panel shows: click “Customize”, tick the tools you want and drag them into order.
- The expanded notch now has “Panel” and “Settings” buttons, so both stay reachable even when the menu-bar icon is hidden.
- Record your own global shortcuts under Settings → General → Shortcuts, one to open the menu-bar panel and one to open Settings. None are set by default.

**Improvements**
- More reliable alerts: sounds and notifications now come straight from the app, so they arrive on time even while the notch is hidden or covered by a full-screen app. Each alert is written to the log.
- The notch and the menu-bar panel only take up the space you can see; no invisible area sits on top of other apps any more.
- Smoother animations: no flash when the notch opens, and no stutter in other apps while “Clear all” burns.
- The welcome animation and setup guide are gone; on first launch, Settings opens on Connections.
- Building from source works with Homebrew’s Node and the bash that ships with macOS.

On an older version: open the menu-bar panel and click “New version” at the bottom to update, or download the dmg below.
