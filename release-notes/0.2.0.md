**新功能**
- 提醒：任务等你或完成时，除了刘海，还能响提示音、发系统通知。提示音可从 13 个原创音效和 macOS 自带的声音里选，默认「问询」（等你）和「三连音」（完成）。
- 提醒的细节都能调：一直没处理时隔一段时间再提醒、暂停提醒 1 小时或到明天早上、每个工具单独开关，以及 Codex 额度和 DeepSeek 余额变低时提醒一次。
- 设置新增「刘海」和「提醒」两页，点「试一下」就能看到刘海弹出、听到声音、收到通知。
- 全屏 App 里刘海可以收起，或只在等你时显示；等你时也能自动弹出。
- 右键点菜单栏的火苗：暂停提醒、打开设置、检查更新、退出。
- 清除完成的任务时有燃烧的声音：单条是短促的一下，全部清除是一阵火焰声。
- 通用页新增：重新看欢迎引导、反馈问题、导出诊断信息（不含对话内容和 Key）、重置设置。
- 浅色模式也是毛玻璃了；设置窗口打开和关闭更顺滑，不再闪一下黑。

**修正**
- DeepSeek Harness 回答很快，或你确认之后跑完时，不显示完成。现在插件会实时上报状态，在跑和完成都显示得更及时。

**升级提示**
- 用 DeepSeek Harness 的话，升级后请重启一次 DeepSeek Harness，新插件才会生效。
- 第一次发系统通知时，macOS 会询问是否允许；也可以在「设置 → 提醒」里点「允许通知」。

已安装旧版本的用户：打开菜单栏面板，底部会出现「有新版本」，点进去下载并安装即可；也可以直接下载下面的 dmg 重新安装。

**New**
- Alerts: besides the notch, Fire Ring can now play a sound and post a system notification when a task needs you or finishes. Pick from 13 original sounds or the macOS ones.
- Fine-tune alerts: remind again if you have not responded, pause for an hour or until tomorrow, per-tool switches, and a one-time alert when Codex usage or the DeepSeek balance runs low.
- New Notch and Alerts pages in Settings, with a “Try it” button that plays the whole alert.
- The notch can hide in full-screen apps, or show only when something needs you; it can also pop open when a task waits for you.
- Right-click the flame in the menu bar to pause alerts, open Settings, check for updates or quit.
- Clearing finished tasks now has a burning sound.
- General: replay the welcome, send feedback, export a diagnostics file (no conversation content or keys), reset settings.
- Light mode is now frosted glass too; the Settings window opens and closes without flashing dark.

**Fix**
- DeepSeek Harness tasks that answered very quickly, or finished after you confirmed something, were not shown as finished. The plugin now reports status live.

**Upgrading**
- If you use DeepSeek Harness, restart it once after updating so the new plugin loads.
- macOS asks once before Fire Ring can post notifications; you can also click “Allow notifications” in Settings → Alerts.

On an older version: open the menu-bar panel and click “New version” at the bottom to update, or download the dmg below.
